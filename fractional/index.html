01 · When it fits

You need an ecommerce executive, not another vendor.

A fractional leader makes sense when the business is big enough that digital decisions move real revenue, but a full-time executive isn't the right hire yet, or isn't hired yet.

  • Online is a big share of revenue, with nobody senior owning itThe site, the marketing and the data each have an owner, but nobody owns the number.
  • A leadership gap after someone leftSomeone needs to steady the team and keep growth going while you hire, or decide whether to.
  • A replatform on the horizonShopify Plus, Salesforce, Adobe Commerce or headless, and no one inside who has led one end to end.
  • Agency and vendor sprawlFive vendors, five invoices, and every change waits in someone else's queue.
  • Numbers nobody trustsReports that disagree, and questions that take a week to answer.
  • A mandate to put AI to workYou want AI in real operations, catalog, QA, analytics and campaigns, not in a slide.

04 · The first 90 days

Useful in week one. A plan by week four.

  1. Weeks 1–2

    Listen and audit

    Time with the CEO, the team and key vendors. An audit of the site, the stack, the data and the spend, and a few quick wins shipped.

  2. Weeks 3–4

    Plan

    A 12-month roadmap with owners and a scorecard, agreed with leadership. What to keep, fix, replace or stop.

  3. Months 2–3

    Run it

    The roadmap in motion: team and vendor changes made, the first big bets under way, and a weekly operating rhythm.

  4. After that

    Build to hand off

    Hire or grow the full-time leader, document how the work runs, and step back to advisory when you're ready.

06 · FAQ

Questions people ask first.

What is a fractional chief digital officer?

A fractional chief digital officer is a senior digital executive who works part-time for a company, usually one to two days a week on a monthly retainer. You get the same strategy, leadership and accountability as a full-time hire, for a fraction of the time and cost.

How is a fractional VP of Ecommerce different from a consultant or an agency?

A consultant or agency delivers a project and leaves. A fractional VP of Ecommerce joins the leadership team, owns the ecommerce number and the roadmap, manages the people and vendors, and stays accountable for results month after month.

What size of business is this for?

Most often brands doing roughly $5M to $150M a year online, DTC, B2B or both, that are past founder-led ecommerce but not ready for, or still hiring, a full-time digital executive. Founder-led, family-owned and investor-backed companies all fit.

How much time do you commit, and how is it priced?

Advisory is a few hours a month, a fractional executive role is typically one to two days a week, and interim cover is most of the week for a set period. Fees are a monthly retainer set by the time commitment, or a fixed fee for project work like a replatform, agreed after a first call.

How long does a fractional engagement last?

Usually six to twelve months: three months to diagnose, plan and get the roadmap moving, then long enough to see results and hire or grow a full-time leader. After that many clients keep a lighter advisory role.

Do you work remotely?

Yes. I'm based in Wilmington, Delaware and work remotely with teams across the US, with on-site time for planning sessions and workshops when it helps.

Which platforms do you know best?

Shopify Plus, Adobe Commerce (Magento), Salesforce and headless storefronts on Next.js, with NetSuite, Klaviyo, Attentive, Snowflake and GA4 around them. I've led migrations onto Sitecore, a headless stack and Shopify Plus.
