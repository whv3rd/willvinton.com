03 · How we build

From sketchbook to something real.

  1. Dream it up

    The kids own the idea: the world, who lives there, what happens.

  2. Write it down

    A world guide and character sheets, so everything stays consistent.

  3. Build it with AI

    Claude, Figma and simple game tools turn the guide into art, stories and playable builds.

  4. Ship it

    Publish it here, share it, and turn the ones people love into apps.

It's the same idea as Springspot: a very small team, the right tools, and a lot more getting made. See Springspot AI

04 · Ground rules

Their ideas.
My job is the rest.

  • The kids own their ideasWorlds and characters are credited to their creator.
  • Privacy firstNo real names, faces, schools or locations. Creators go by a studio name.
  • A parent runs the accountsPublishing, app stores, comments and anything with money go through me.
  • It can earn, but it's for fun firstIf something takes off, it can become an app or pay for itself. If it doesn't, we still made something.
